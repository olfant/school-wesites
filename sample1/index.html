Educational Mission

Building Knowledge, Values & Confidence.

Situated in Haldipokhar, Potka block of East Singhbhum, Jharkhand, CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR serves as an intellectual cornerstone for secondary and higher secondary education in our region.

The school focuses on quality education, good values, discipline, life skills and the all-round development of students. Under the curriculum framework of the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), our dedicated teaching faculty nurtures students across foundational streams—Arts, Science, and Commerce—while upholding civic duties and cultural heritage.
