Science & Art Exhibitions

Showcasing creative inventions, working STEM models, and ecological sustainability projects.

Student Innovation

Science, Craft & Project Exhibitions

Every year, the school organizes an expansive exhibition platform showcasing working science models, sustainable agricultural solutions, robotics prototypes, and creative arts.

The exhibition provides students a platform to explain scientific phenomena to visiting parents, teachers, and peers. Working in teams, students design functional apparatus addressing regional challenges such as soil health, rainwater conservation, and clean rural energy.

Renewable Energy Models

Solar cooker prototypes, miniature wind generators, and solar-powered drip irrigation circuits constructed by +2 Science students.

Water Conservation & Ecology
