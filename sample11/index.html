ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

A PLACE WHERE EVERY JOURNEY BEGINS.

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is dedicated to delivering high quality secondary and higher secondary education that blends academic rigor with moral grounding and real-world practical skills.

Located at हल्दीपोखर, पोटका, पूर्वी सिंहभूम (झारखण्ड), our campus provides students with state-of-the-art facilities, supportive faculty, science laboratories, vocational training labs, and a vibrant co-curricular environment.

  • Quality Education as a School of Excellence
  • Good Values, Integrity and Ethical Conduct
  • Strict Discipline and Purposeful Learning
  • Essential Life Skills & Vocational Readiness
  • Holistic Development: Sports, Culture & Scouts

ENROLLMENT GUIDE

YOUR FUTURE STARTS WITH A STEP.

Explore admission guidelines for CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR. Our transparent five-stage roadmap prepares every prospective student.

1

Admission Information

Review eligibility criteria, stream subjects and official notification released by the education department.

2

Obtain Application Form

Collect official admission application forms from the school administrative office or download instructions.

3

Submit Documents

Submit completed forms with transfer certificate, marksheets, caste/residence certificates and passport photos.

4

Document Verification

Verification of submitted original certificates by the School Management and Admission Committee.

5

Admission Confirmation

Final selection announcement, roll assignment, and issuance of student ID with academic orientation.
