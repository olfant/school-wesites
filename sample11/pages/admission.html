Admission Procedure

Home / Admission
Official Admission Disclaimer

Admissions to secondary and higher secondary (+2) classes at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR are conducted strictly pursuant to official notification guidelines issued by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand. This website does not imply admissions are currently active or grant automatic enrollment. Please verify dates, seat reservations and criteria with the school administrative office.

5-STAGE ROADMAP

The Admission Journey

A transparent, step-by-step procedure outlining prospective student admission into our institution.

1

Admission Notice

Publication of official admission advertisement, stream availability, seat matrix, and eligibility rules.

2

Form Distribution

Collection of application forms from the school admission desk during designated office hours.

3

Document Filing

Submission of completed application form along with verified academic and identity documents.

4

Certificate Check

Scrutiny of original certificates, transfer certificate (TC), mark sheets, and category verification.

5

Final Enrollment

Confirmation notification, roll allocation, issuance of student ID, and student induction orientation.

CHECKLIST

Required Documents

Applicants must bring self-attested photocopies along with original documents for in-person verification by the Admission Committee:

  • Original School Leaving Certificate / Transfer Certificate (SLC/TC).
  • Marksheet of previous qualifying examination (Class 8 / Class 10 Board).
  • Character / Conduct Certificate from head of previous institution.
  • Caste Certificate (SC/ST/OBC) issued by competent authority (if applicable).
  • Residential / Domicile Certificate of Jharkhand.
  • Copy of Student's Aadhaar Card and Bank Account passbook details.
  • Recent passport-size photographs (4 copies).

Frequently Asked Questions

We offer Higher Secondary streams in Science (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science), Arts (History, Pol Science, Economics, Geography, Sociology), and Commerce (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics).

The administrative office operates Monday through Saturday between 09:00 AM and 04:00 PM (except gazetted state holidays).

Forms must be collected directly from the school campus at Haldipokhar, Potka upon release of the official notification by the education department.
