Subject Selection Guidelines

Students entering Class 11 choose one core academic stream based on their Class 10 qualifying marks and personal career goals. Subject change requests are permitted strictly within the first month of enrollment in accordance with JAC council directives.

Vocational & Skill Courses

In line with the National Education Policy and Jharkhand School of Excellence guidelines, students may also opt for vocational skill modules including Information Technology (IT/ITeS), electronics basics, and agriculture practical skills.
