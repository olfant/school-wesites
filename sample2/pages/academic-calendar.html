ANNUAL ROADMAP

Academic Calendar & Important Dates

Chronological schedule of terms, internal evaluations, JAC board examination milestones, national celebrations, and seasonal recesses.

Institutional Notice: Official calendar timelines adhere to notifications published by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, and Jharkhand Academic Council, Ranchi.

Term I: April – June

New Session
  • Apr
    Commencement of New Academic Session: Classroom allotment, distribution of state textbooks, and parent orientation for Class 9 and +2 entrants.
  • May
    Summer Co-Curricular Week & Pre-Vacation Work: Project assignments, tree plantation drives on campus, and holiday homework distribution.
  • Jun
    Re-opening Post Summer Recess & Diagnostic Tests: Review of student vacation projects and commencement of first periodic assessment.

Term II: July – September

Formative Stage
  • Jul
    First Periodic Assessments & Science Club Formation: Conduct of unit examinations across secondary and higher secondary batches.
  • Aug
    Independence Day Celebrations (15th August): Ceremonial flag hoisting, patriotic song presentations, speech contest, and Scout & Guide parade.
  • Sep
    Teachers' Day (5th Sept) & Mid-Term Evaluations: Student-led felicitation of faculty and conduct of Mid-Term Terminal Examinations.

Term III: October – December

Festive & Exhibitions
  • Oct
    Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti & Autumn Recess: Cleanliness drive (Swachhata Pakhwada) and autumn festival holidays.
  • Nov
    Annual Science Exhibition & Jharkhand Statehood Day: Student model displays, tribal cultural showcase, and regional heritage celebration.
  • Dec
    Annual Athletic Sports Meet & Winter Preparation: Track races, inter-house volleyball, and revision classes for Classes 10 & 12.

Term IV: January – March

Board Examinations
  • Jan
    Pre-Board Examinations & Republic Day (26th Jan): Pre-matric and pre-intermediate examinations; ceremonial parade and patriotic arts.
  • Feb
    JAC Annual Board Examinations: Formal conduct of Class 10 (Secondary) and Class 12 (Higher Secondary) State Board Examinations.
  • Mar
    Annual Results Declaration & Session Wrap: Evaluation of home examinations for Classes 9 & 11 and commencement of new admissions.
