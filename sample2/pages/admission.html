Admission Process & Guidelines

Information on admissions for Secondary (Classes 9 & 10) and Higher Secondary (+2 Science, Arts, and Commerce) streams at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR.

State Government Admission Guidelines: Admissions are conducted in accordance with the annual notifications and reservation policies mandated by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand.
1

Notification Release

Annual notification published on school notice board following declaration of JAC Board results.

2

Form Submission

Collection and submission of completed application forms along with required academic credentials.

3

Document Verification

Scrutiny of original transfer certificates (SLC/CLC), marks-sheets, and category certificates by the committee.

4

Enrollment & Roll Allotment

Final admission approval, section allocation, issue of student ID, and orientation program.

CHECKLIST

Mandatory Documents Required

Applicants seeking admission to Class 9 or +2 (Class 11) must submit self-attested photocopies along with original documents for in-person verification:

  • Original School Leaving Certificate (SLC) / College Leaving Certificate (CLC).
  • Mark-sheet of Previous Qualifying Examination (Class 8 / Class 10 Board).
  • Character Certificate from the Head of the previous institution.
  • Copy of Aadhaar Card of student and parent.
  • Caste & Residential Certificate (if applicable for reservation benefits).
  • Recent passport-sized color photographs (4 copies).
