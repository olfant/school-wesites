### 2. Design Concept — Modern Swiss Grid Edition
This website is **Version 5** of the educational design series, engineered with:
- **Modular 12-Column Mathematical Swiss Grid**: Clean architectural alignment, hairline borders (`#D9E5DC`), and corner registration marks (`+`).
- **Minimalist Typographic Hierarchy**: Editorial oversized headings utilizing Google Fonts *Plus Jakarta Sans* and *Space Grotesk* combined with *Noto Sans Devanagari* for authentic Hindi lettering.
- **Recognizable Green Identity**:
  - Primary Green: `#105A34`
  - Dark Green: `#073E28`
  - Deep Green: `#042D1C`
  - Accent Green: `#16864A`
  - Fresh Green: `#4AA875`
  - Pale Green: `#E8F3EA`
  - Light Green: `#F3F8F4`
  - White: `#FFFFFF`
  - Main Text: `#1D3024`
  - Secondary Text: `#647268`
  - Hairline Border: `#D9E5DC`
- **Interactive Student Development Journey**: Connecting 6 developmental stages (Learning, Understanding, Creativity, Collaboration, Responsibility, Growth) via a progressive horizontal desktop timeline and responsive vertical mobile timeline.
- **Data Accuracy Protocol**: Real verified school information is maintained strictly throughout. No Principal names, establishment years, student counts, or examination marks have been fabricated; clearly labeled official placeholders are used where official records are pending or maintained in offline school registers.

### 4. Complete List of 20 Functional Pages
1. `index.html` — Homepage featuring all 15 dedicated Swiss sections (Hero, Identity, Directory, Philosophy, Campus, Principal Quote, Academics, Learning Journey, Activities, Notices, Results, Gallery, Admission, Contact, Footer).

3. `pages/principal.html` — Leadership address, educational vision, and administrative office hours.
4. `pages/mission-vision.html` — Institutional mission, vision statement, and core ethical values.
5. `pages/smc-members.html` — School Management Committee statutory mandate, responsibilities, and responsive members table.
6. `pages/academic-overview.html` — Pedagogical architecture, secondary and +2 curriculum standards under JAC Ranchi.
7. `pages/classes-subjects.html` — Detailed breakdown of Secondary Classes 9–10 and +2 Arts, Science, and Commerce streams.
8. `pages/academic-calendar.html` — Monthly scholastic roadmap, assessment intervals, and holiday observances.
9. `pages/teaching-learning.html` — Classroom methodology, science practical labs, peer study circles, and remedial support.
10. `pages/achievements.html` — Scholastic honors, sports championship trophies, cultural citations, and Scout honors.
11. `pages/cultural-activities.html` — Jharkhand folk dance traditions (Santhali, Chhau, Jhumar), musical ensembles, and national commemorations.
12. `pages/sports-activities.html` — Athletics track & field disciplines, volleyball, football, kho-kho, and annual sports meet.
13. `pages/exhibitions.html` — Annual Science Exhibition, renewable energy working models, eco-innovations, and indigenous art displays.
14. `pages/skills-competition.html` — Debates, speech elocution, quiz leagues, Olympiad logic, and mental arithmetic.
15. `pages/vocational-activities.html` — Applied agriculture, nursery grafting, electrical circuitry basics, and digital ICT literacy.
16. `pages/results.html` — Examination results directory with live session, exam, and class filters, gazettes, and official disclaimers.
17. `pages/admission.html` — 5-step illustrative admission guide, eligibility criteria, document checklist, and FAQ accordion.
18. `pages/scout-guide.html` — Bharat Scouts & Guides troop drills, pioneering, knotcraft, and Governor Award (Rajya Puraskar) pathway.
19. `pages/gallery.html` — Filterable visual archive with interactive modal Lightbox, prev/next controls, and captions.
20. `pages/contact.html` — Institutional contact directory, counter timings, location map guide, and validated inquiry form.
