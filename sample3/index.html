01 / WHO WE ARE

Learning for Life.
Growing with Values.

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is dedicated to empowering students of Haldipokhar and Potka Block with wholesome secondary and senior secondary education under the Jharkhand Academic Council.

We integrate core academic rigor with values, life skills, physical training, and practical exposure. We believe every child carries immense potential that thrives through disciplined mentorship and cooperative learning.
