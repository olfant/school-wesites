About Our School

Discover the foundation, values, educational philosophy and institutional environment of +2 Rajkiyakrit Vidya Niketan High School, Haldipokhar.

01 / PROFILE & CONTEXT

Rooted in Heritage. Committed to Progress.

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is a premier state government secondary and senior secondary institution located in Haldipokhar, within the Potka block of East Singhbhum district, Jharkhand.

The school caters to a vibrant and diverse student body comprising students from rural, agrarian, and tribal communities across Potka block. Affiliated with the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi, the school upholds rigorous academic delivery while instilling values of civic duty, self-reliance, and mutual respect.

02 / BACKGROUND

Institutional History & Establishment

Understanding the origin and public recognition of the institution as a pillar of learning in Haldipokhar.

Public Transformation into Rajkiyakrit High School

The school originated as a community-driven initiative by dedicated local educationists to ensure secondary education was accessible to students of Haldipokhar without requiring long travel to distant sub-divisional towns. Recognizing its enduring service and educational contribution, the Government of Jharkhand officially designated the school as a Rajkiyakrit (State-Administered) institution.

Later, the institution was upgraded to the +2 Higher Secondary level, offering Intermediate programs in Arts, Science, and Commerce to cater to matriculated students seeking tertiary preparatory education within their own block.
