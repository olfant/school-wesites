Admission Desk

Procedures, eligibility guidelines, documentation requirements, and frequently asked questions for incoming students.

Official State Policy Notice: As a government secondary and senior secondary institution, admission seats, reservation norms (SC, ST, OBC, EWS, Divyangjan), and application schedules are administered as per directives issued by the Department of School Education & Literacy, Govt. of Jharkhand. Exact cutoff dates and seat availability must be confirmed directly at the school office counter.
01 / ENROLLMENT STEPS

Five-Step Admission Procedure

01

Obtain Information

Review eligibility criteria, stream options (Arts, Science, Commerce), and document requirements from notice boards.

02

Collect Form

Procure the official application form from the school office counter during working hours (9:30 AM – 3:30 PM).

03

Submit Documents

Submit completed application forms along with self-attested photocopies of marksheets and identity proofs.

04

Verification

Admission scrutiny committee validates original Transfer Certificates (TC), Aadhaar cards, and previous report cards.

05

Confirmation

Roll number assignment, fee receipt issuance (as per state rules), and enrollment into school registers.

02 / ELIGIBILITY

Eligibility Criteria

Class 9 (Secondary Entry):

Successful completion of Class 8 from a recognized government or affiliated school, with valid Transfer Certificate (TC) and passing progress report.

Class 11 (+2 Intermediate Entry):

Candidates who have passed the JAC Secondary Examination (Matriculation) or equivalent recognized state/central board (CBSE, ICSE) are eligible for Arts, Science, or Commerce stream based on seat quotas and subject prerequisites.

03 / DOCUMENTATION

Mandatory Document Checklist

  • Original School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (TC) signed by previous Headmaster.
  • Self-attested copy of Previous Class Marksheet / JAC Matriculation Marks Gazette.
  • Copy of Student Aadhaar Card and Parent/Guardian Aadhaar Card.
  • Caste & Income Certificate issued by competent authority (for reserved quota/scholarship).
  • Copy of Student Bank Account Passbook (for government DBT/scholarship disbursements).
  • Recent passport-size color photographs (4 copies).

Frequently Asked Questions
