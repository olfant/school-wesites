Classes & Subjects

Curriculum streams, subject allocations, and academic structure from Class 9 through Senior Secondary (+2).

01 / SECONDARY WING

Secondary Curriculum (Classes 9 & 10)

Conducted under the secondary examination regulations of the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi. The curriculum emphasizes broad-based literacy, scientific methodology, numeracy, and linguistic mastery.

Compulsory Subjects

  • Hindi (Course A / B): Language literature, grammar, and composition.
  • English Language & Literature: Prose, poetry, reading comprehension, writing.
  • Mathematics: Arithmetic, algebra, geometry, trigonometry, statistics.
  • Science: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Theory & Practical).
  • Social Science: History, Geography, Democratic Politics, and Economics.

Language Electives & Co-Scholastic

  • Additional Language: Sanskrit / Santhali / Bengali / Ho (As per candidate choice & state list).
  • Physical & Health Education: Sports drills, yoga, personal health, and sanitation.
  • Work Experience / Art Education: Craft, drawing, eco-club, and vocational basics.
  • Moral & Value Education: Weekly ethical discussions and assembly pledges.
02 / SENIOR SECONDARY

+2 Intermediate Streams (Classes 11 & 12)

Our senior secondary wing offers three specialized academic streams leading to the Higher Secondary Certificate awarded by JAC Ranchi.

STREAM / 01

+2 Inter Arts

Critical humanities, social analysis, history, and civic inquiry.

Subject Combinations:
  • Core Hindi or Core English
  • History (इतिहास)
  • Political Science (राजनीति विज्ञान)
  • Geography (भूगोल)
  • Economics / Sociology
  • Optional / Elective Language
STREAM / 02

+2 Inter Science

Theoretical rigor and experimental practicals in natural sciences.

Subject Combinations:
  • Core English or Core Hindi
  • Physics (भौतिकी - Theory & Practical)
  • Chemistry (रसायन शास्त्र - Theory & Practical)
  • Mathematics (गणित)
  • Biology (जीव विज्ञान - Botany & Zoology)
  • Computer Science / Additional
STREAM / 03

+2 Inter Commerce

Business numeracy, accountancy, economics, and commercial applications.

Subject Combinations:
  • Core Hindi or Core English
  • Accountancy (लेखाशास्त्र)
  • Business Studies (व्यवसाय अध्ययन)
  • Commercial Arithmetic / Economics
  • Entrepreneurship (उद्यमिता)
  • Computer Application / Additional
