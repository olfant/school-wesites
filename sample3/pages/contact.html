How to Reach the Campus:

Haldipokhar is located in Potka Block, East Singhbhum district, along the state highway route connecting Jamshedpur to Odisha border. Local passenger buses, auto-rickshaws, and road transport connect Jamshedpur (Tatanagar) to Haldipokhar market, from where the school campus is within walking distance.
