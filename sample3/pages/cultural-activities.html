Cultural Activities

Preserving regional folk traditions, celebrating national festivals, and cultivating artistic self-expression.

01 / CULTURAL TRADITION

Rhythm, Heritage, and Creative Expression

The rich cultural fabric of East Singhbhum and Jharkhand is woven deeply into school life at +2 RVNHS Haldipokhar.

Cultural celebrations allow students to explore their artistic talents in traditional folk dance, tribal music, classical singing, dramatic stage productions, and poetic recitation, fostering cultural pride and inter-community camaraderie.

DANCE TRADITIONS
Santhali, Jhumar, Chhau & Paika
MUSIC & ENSEMBLE
Mandar, Dholak, Flute & Choral
Students performing indigenous Jharkhand folk dances during annual celebrations.
02 / ANNUAL EVENTS

Institutional Celebrations & National Days

EVENT / 01

National Day Commemorations

Independence Day (15 August) and Republic Day (26 January) feature tricolor flag hoisting, national anthem, parade drills, patriotic songs, and speeches.

PATRIOTIC PRIDE
EVENT / 02

Birsa Munda Jayanti & State Day

Celebrated on 15 November in honor of Bhagwan Birsa Munda and the creation of Jharkhand state, highlighting tribal history, values, and freedom struggles.

HERITAGE HONORS
EVENT / 03

Annual School Cultural Fest

Multi-day festival featuring solo singing, group folk dance, one-act social plays, traditional costume parades, and literary recitation competitions.

CREATIVE SHOWCASE
03 / LITERARY & ART

Literary & Performing Clubs

01 — Hindi Sahitya Sabha Weekly poetry recitation, story writing, Hindi Diwas competitions, and literary debates.
02 — Tribal Heritage & Folk Club Preservation of local folk tales, traditional musical instruments, and indigenous oral histories.
03 — Drama & Social Awareness Troop Nukkad Natak (street plays) addressing literacy, cleanliness, water conservation, and anti-superstition.
