Headmaster / In-Charge Principal • +2 RVNHS Haldipokhar
Welcome to the official portal of CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR, हल्दीपोखर. Our school represents more than just a physical campus; it is a sanctum where knowledge, moral values, and civic duty converge to prepare the youth of Potka block for a purposeful life.

Our Educational Vision

In rural and semi-urban regions, quality schooling is the single most powerful equalizer. Our commitment is to provide equal, rigorous, and inspiring academic opportunities for all boys and girls. We strive to look beyond marks to develop analytical capability, linguistic proficiency, scientific inquisitiveness, and empathetic community involvement.

Message to Students

Dear students, discipline is the vital bridge connecting your ambitions with real achievement. Value your classroom hours, question constructively, participate actively in sports, cultural stages, and Scout & Guide activities. Strive for self-discipline, respect your parents and teachers, and remain committed to truth and continuous self-improvement.

Message to Parents & Community

A child’s progress depends upon the triangle of trust between student, parent, and teacher. We encourage every parent to monitor regular attendance, engage actively with our School Management Committee (SMC), and maintain an encouraging atmosphere at home for study and reading.

Data Transparency Policy:
In strict adherence to verified institutional reporting standards, administrative appointments and officially notified Principal designations are preserved in certified gazette records and official school registers.
