Bharat Scouts & Guides

Building character, discipline, self-reliance, emergency first aid skills, and selfless civic leadership.

01 / TROOP VALUES

"Duty to God, Duty to Country, Duty to Others."

The Bharat Scouts & Guides unit at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR, हल्दीपोखर is one of the most active co-curricular youth formations in Potka block.

Through regular weekly drills, patrol system governance, knotcraft, pioneering, outdoor camps, and community hygiene initiatives, our Scouts and Guides learn to be self-reliant, courageous, and ready to serve society in times of need.

TROOP MOTTO
तैयार रहो (Be Prepared)
ACCREDITATION
BSG Jharkhand State
Scouts practicing flag ceremony protocol, compass navigation, and knotcraft.
02 / TRAINING DOMAINS

Core Scout Training Areas

TRAINING / 01

Drill & Flag Protocol

March-past cadence, color guard honors, national flag hoisting protocols, whistle signaling, and smart ceremonial turnout during school and national holidays.

DISCIPLINED CADENCE
TRAINING / 02

Pioneering, Knots & Camping

Mastering reef knot, clove hitch, timber hitch, square lashing, tent pitching, compass navigation, star reading, and outdoor campsite construction.

SURVIVAL SKILLS
TRAINING / 03

First Aid & Civic Service

Bandaging wounds, triangular sling application, stretcher-making, traffic assistance during local fairs, village cleanliness drives, and disaster volunteer training.

COMMUNITY SERVICE
03 / ADVANCEMENT

Scout Badge Advancement Hierarchy

01 — Pravesh (Entry Level) Understanding Scout Law, Promise, Motto, Sign, Salute, Flag Song, and National Anthem.
02 — Pratham Sopan Knotcraft, patrol signaling, compass bearings, personal hygiene habits, and daily good turns.
03 — Dwitiya Sopan First aid procedures, camp cooking without utensils, tracking signs, pioneering lashings, and hike expeditions.
04 — Tritiya Sopan Advanced map reading, swimming / physical endurance, night observation, and leadership of a patrol troop.
05 — Rajya Puraskar (Governor Award) State-level testing camp recognition signed by the Hon'ble Governor of Jharkhand for exemplary Scout leadership.
