Sports & Physical Education

Physical vitality, sportsmanship, athletics excellence, and teamwork on campus grounds.

01 / ATHLETIC PHILOSOPHY

Strength, Stamina & Sportsmanship

Physical health is the foundation of intellectual achievement. At +2 RVNHS Haldipokhar, our sports curriculum encourages every student to participate in daily physical conditioning and competitive athletics.

With open campus grounds, students regularly practice track and field events, volleyball tournaments, kho-kho matches, kabaddi, and football, participating actively in sub-divisional, block, and district level school meets.

TRACK & FIELD
100m, 400m, Relay & Jumps
TEAM SPORTS
Volleyball, Football & Kho-Kho
Campus athletics facilities promoting healthy competition, team spirit, and stamina.
02 / DISCIPLINES

Athletic Programs & Games

DISCIPLINE / 01

Track & Field Athletics

Sprint heats (100m, 200m), middle-distance races (400m, 800m), 4x100m inter-house relays, running long jump, and shot put throws.

INDIVIDUAL EXCELLENCE
DISCIPLINE / 02

Volleyball & Football

Full-size volleyball court with net fixtures and football matches training students in cooperative tactics, endurance, and game discipline.

TEAM COORDINATION
DISCIPLINE / 03

Kho-Kho & Kabaddi

Traditional Indian sports fostering swift reflex response, agility, breath control, strategic tagging, and physical fortitude among boys and girls.

AGILITY & ENDURANCE
03 / ANNUAL MEET

Annual Sports Meet Format

01 — Inter-House Division Students are organized into competitive school houses to foster belonging, team loyalty, and democratic leadership.
02 — Selection Trials Fair, measured qualifying rounds supervised by physical education instructors ensure merit-based participation in finals.
03 — Grand Sports Day Opening march-past salute, athletic heats, tug-of-war, teacher-parent novelty race, and formal certificate distribution.
