Vocational Activities

Hands-on practical skills, agricultural techniques, electrical safety, and digital tool competencies.

01 / APPLIED LEARNING

Bridging Classroom Learning with Practical Life Skills

In alignment with the National Education Policy and state vocational skill guidelines, our school places heavy emphasis on experiential, hands-on learning that instills dignity of labor and practical readiness.

Students learn essential life skills including agricultural botany, nursery maintenance, drip irrigation principles, household electrical repairs, and digital information literacy, preparing them to be resourceful and employable contributors.

APPLIED AGRICULTURE
Plant Nursery & Soil Health
PRACTICAL TECH
Electrical Circuits & Digital ICT
Practical training in measurement, electronics, agriculture, and tool safety.
02 / TRAINING MODULES

Core Practical Skill Modules

MODULE / 01

Agriculture & Plantation

Seedling care, organic compost formulation, nursery sapling grafting, drip irrigation pipe layouts, and kitchen gardening on school grounds.

AGRARIAN SKILLS
MODULE / 02

Electrical Basics & Safety

Understanding voltage and wattage, circuit breakers, domestic fuse wiring, plug fitting, earthing protocols, and household electrical maintenance.

TECHNICAL SAFETY
MODULE / 03

Digital Literacy & ICT

Familiarity with computers, keyboard typing, document drafting, data entry spreadsheets, cyber hygiene, and online educational resources.

DIGITAL PREPAREDNESS
03 / VALUE INTEGRATION

Dignity of Labor & Self-Reliance

01 — Campus Shramdaan Voluntary student and teacher campus cleaning drives, tree planting, and recycling efforts fostering mutual humility.
02 — Craft & Waste Upcycling Creating functional dustbins, paper bags, and instructional aids from discarded carton boxes and plastic containers.
03 — Career Orientation Inviting technical experts, local craftsmen, and agriculture extension officers to mentor senior secondary students.
