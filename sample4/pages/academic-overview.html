Academic Programs & Overview

Comprehensive curriculum, secondary education, +2 higher secondary streams, and pedagogical approach.

Grades 9 & 10

Secondary School Program

The Secondary curriculum builds a broad, rigorous academic base across essential disciplines prescribed by the Jharkhand Academic Council. It prepares students for the Matriculation Examination while encouraging analytical inquiry and disciplined study habits.

Mathematics Science (Phy/Chem/Bio) Social Science Hindi Language English Language Sanskrit / Regional

Pedagogy

Teaching Methodology

Combining traditional structured instruction with active modern practical engagement.

Concept-First Instruction

Breaking down abstract formulas into understandable everyday phenomena through bilingual explanation and blackboard clarity.

Hands-on Laboratory Work

Practical experiments for secondary and +2 science students verifying physical laws, chemical reactions and biological specimens.

Formative Assessment

Regular unit tests, quarterly evaluations, pre-board examinations and marks analysis guiding personalized remedial support.

State Council Affiliation

Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi

All secondary and higher secondary curriculums, registration protocols, examination gazettes, and academic evaluation calendars strictly adhere to the official notifications of the Jharkhand Academic Council and Department of School Education and Literacy, Jharkhand.
