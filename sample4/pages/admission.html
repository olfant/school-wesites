Admission Guidelines & Process

Eligibility, application steps, required documentation, and guidelines for Secondary (Class 9) and +2 Higher Secondary (Class 11).

Official Admission Policy Note

Admissions for Class IX (Secondary) and Class XI (+2 Higher Secondary in Science, Arts, Commerce) are governed strictly by the regulations of the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, and the Jharkhand Academic Council. Interested parents and candidates must obtain verified application forms directly from the school administrative office.

Step-by-Step

Admission Procedure

A transparent and streamlined process for prospective candidates and their guardians.

01

Notice & Form Issuance

Official notification issued following publication of Class 8 or Class 10 results. Collect the official registration form from the administrative desk.

02

Document Verification

Submit the duly filled form along with certified photocopies of previous marksheet, Transfer Certificate (TC/SLC), and Aadhaar credentials.

03

Merit & Stream Allotment

For +2 Higher Secondary, stream allocation (Science, Arts, Commerce) is determined based on qualifying marks and state reservation criteria.

04

Enrollment & Roll Issuance

Final enrollment entry into the school student register, issuance of institutional roll number, identity card, and textbooks.

Eligibility Norms

Eligibility Criteria

  • Secondary Admission (Class IX): Candidate must have successfully passed Class VIII from a recognized government or aided educational institution with valid Transfer Certificate.
  • +2 Higher Secondary Admission (Class XI): Candidate must have cleared the Secondary / Matriculation Examination conducted by JAC, CBSE, ICSE, or any recognized State Council.
  • Age Criteria: As prescribed by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand.
Documentation Check

Mandatory Documents Required

  • Original School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (SLC / TC)
  • Certified Marksheet of qualifying examination (Class 8 / Class 10)
  • Admit Card of Secondary Examination (for Class 11 candidates)
  • Photocopy of Student's Aadhaar Card & Parent's ID
  • Caste & Income Certificate (if applicable for state scholarship welfare)
  • 4 recent passport-size colored photographs
  • Student Bank Account Passbook copy for direct DBT welfare benefits
