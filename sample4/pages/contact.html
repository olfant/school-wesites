Direction Guide

How to Reach Campus

The school is located in Haldipokhar within Potka Block of East Singhbhum district, approximately 25 km south of Jamshedpur (Tatanagar) along the state highway toward Chaibasa / Odisha border.

Rail Connectivity

Haldipokhar Railway Station (HLP) on the Tatanagar–Badampahar line is located nearby.

Road & Bus Transit

Frequent local bus transit and auto services available from Tatanagar Bus Stand to Haldipokhar Chowk.
