Innovation Fair

Scientific Inventions & Models

Transforming textbook science into working experimental prototypes.

Applied Creativity

Science & Craft Fairs

Every year, +2 विद्या निकेतन organizes institutional science, art, and environmental exhibitions. Students from Classes IX through XII conceptualize and build functional models demonstrating physical principles, renewable energy solutions, and agricultural efficiencies.

Key Exhibition Themes:

Solar Irrigation Systems, Rainwater Harvesting, Rural Waste Management, Organic Farming Models, and Regional Handicrafts.

Renewable Energy Models

Student-constructed solar water heaters, mini windmill generators, and energy-conserving smart streetlamp circuits.

Eco-Water Conservation

Working prototypes of rooftop rainwater collection, groundwater recharge pits, and drip irrigation systems for rural farms.

Art, Craft & Heritage

Clay sculptures, tribal Sohrai and Kohbar paintings, recycled waste crafts, and mechanical wooden models.

Competition Pathway

District & State Level Science Contests

Outstanding exhibits selected at the school level are mentored for nomination to the Potka Block Science Congress and East Singhbhum District Science Fairs organized by the state education department.
