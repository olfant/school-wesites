School Management Committee (SMC / SMDC)

Democratic governance, community oversight and development council of +2 विद्या निकेतन उच्च विद्यालय।

Community Governance

Role of the School Management Committee

As per the Right to Education Act and Secondary Education Development guidelines of the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, the School Management Committee (SMC) and School Management & Development Committee (SMDC) oversee campus infrastructure, student welfare, academic monitoring, and institutional transparency.

The committee comprises elected parent representatives, educational experts, senior faculty members, local panchayat nominees, and the Head of the Institution as Ex-Officio Member Secretary.

Democratic Oversight

Ensuring community-led school development and optimal fund utilization.
