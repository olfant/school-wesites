How to Reach the Campus

By Railway

Nearest major junction is Tatanagar Railway Station (Jamshedpur), located approximately 25-30 km away. Haldipokhar Railway Station also connects local passenger services.

By Road & Bus Transit

Regular bus and shared auto services operate from Jamshedpur to Haldipokhar / Potka Block. The school is centrally accessible along the main thoroughfare.

Local Landmark

Located in Vniketan vicinity in Haldipokhar, easily identified by the School of Excellence administrative gate.
