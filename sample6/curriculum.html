Syllabus Standards

NCERT & JCERT Prescribed Framework

Our school follows textbooks, learning materials, and practical manuals published and approved by JCERT (Jharkhand) and NCERT.

In conformity with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), our lesson plans incorporate multi-disciplinary problem solving, mathematical reasoning, vocational exposure, environmental education, and interactive smart board demonstrations.

Standardized Textbooks

Free distribution of approved state syllabi books to all enrolled pupils.
