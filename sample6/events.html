School Events & Activities

Nurturing leadership, artistic expression, athletic vigor, and community pride through annual school gatherings.

18
Dec
Sports Meet

Annual Sports Championship & Athletic Meet

School Playground 9:00 AM – 4:00 PM

Inter-house athletic sprint races (100m, 200m, 400m), relay championships, long jump, shot put, football cup, and volleyball finals.

28
Feb
National Science Day

Science & Innovation Model Exhibition

School Multipurpose Hall 10:00 AM – 3:30 PM

Student scientific models on solar energy, rainwater conservation, robotics, and biological diversity judged by district educationists.

15
Nov
Statehood Gala

Jharkhand Foundation Day Cultural Gala

Central Campus Courtyard 8:30 AM – 1:30 PM

Honoring tribal freedom fighters (Birsa Munda, Sidhu-Kanhu), traditional folk song ensembles, Chhau and Santhali dance presentations.

15
Aug
National Celebration

Independence Day Flag Hoisting & Parade

School Assembly Grounds 8:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Ceremonial hoisting of the National Tricolour by the Principal, march past parade by student houses, patriotic songs, and sweet distribution.

05
Sep
Teachers' Day & Honors

Annual Academic Honors & Teachers' Day

Main Auditorium 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM

Felicitation of faculty members, awarding certificates of distinction to state board toppers, and student cultural performances.

26
Jan
Republic Day

Republic Day Constitutional Assembly

Campus Grounds 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Preamble recitation, parade inspection, speech on fundamental duties and constitutional ideals, and community sports fellowship.
