Institutional Profile

About Our Institution

CM SOE UPG GOVT +2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is a flagship government institution located in Potka Block, East Singhbhum, Jharkhand.

As part of the prestigious Schools of Excellence (SOE) initiative instituted by the Government of Jharkhand, our school delivers transformative education that combines academic rigor with character building, digital pedagogy, and inclusive community empowerment.

Equipped with modern ICT smart classrooms, cutting-edge science and computer laboratories, expansive library resources, and dedicated sports infrastructure, we provide students from all backgrounds the platform to excel nationally and globally.

Guiding Principles

Our Mission, Vision & Core Values

"Building Knowledge, Character and a Brighter Future." Guided by integrity, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to public educational excellence.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality, and holistic education that fosters critical thinking, technological proficiency, civic responsibility, and moral integrity among all students.

Our Vision

To emerge as a benchmark government educational institution where every child, irrespective of socio-economic background, discovers their potential and leads with distinction.

Our Core Values

Integrity, academic diligence, scientific temperament, inclusivity, discipline, and community service form the foundation of our institution's daily life.
