School Infrastructure

Engineered for safety, accessibility, and high-impact learning under the Jharkhand School of Excellence benchmark.

Campus Facilities

State-of-the-Art Campus Amenities

Every facility has been constructed and upgraded in accordance with national safety and educational standards.

Permanent Campus

1. School Building & Block

Multi-storey permanent architectural structure housing administrative wings, classrooms, staff common rooms, and conference rooms.

Ergonomic

2. Modern Classrooms

Spacious, naturally cross-ventilated rooms with dual-desk ergonomic seating, optimal illumination, and clean acoustic setups.

Digital Board

3. Smart Classrooms

Interactive flat panels with touchscreen digital styluses, multimedia educational content, and high-resolution soundbars.

Networked ICT

4. Computer Laboratory

Modern PC workstations loaded with Python, Scratch, office productivity software, and secure high-speed broadband connectivity.

Precision Optics

5. Physics Laboratory

Specialized optical benches, galvanometers, resistance coils, sonometers, and digital measuring tools for Class 11 and 12 JAC practicals.

Fume Safety

6. Chemistry Laboratory

Acid-resistant workstations, standard chemical reagents, pipettes, burettes, Bunsen burners, and emergency eyewash safety gear.

Compound Microscopes

7. Biology Laboratory

High-magnification microscopes, permanent histological slides, plant and animal specimens, and human anatomical chart displays.

Curated Collection

8. Central School Library

Comprehensive reference collections, encyclopedias, NCERT textbooks, competitive exam guides, national dailies, and periodicals.

Outdoor Sports

9. Sports Playground

Expansive open grounds equipped for football, cricket, volleyball, athletic sprints, and morning assembly parades.

100% RO Safe

10. RO Drinking Water Facility

Centralized Reverse Osmosis purification water stations with continuous microbiological testing and multiple hygiene taps.

Hygienic Blocks

11. Sanitation Facilities

Separate, clean toilet blocks for boys, girls, and Children with Special Needs (CWSN), with continuous water and soap stations.

Multi-Discipline

12. Sports Facilities & Kit

Dedicated athletic gear, cricket kits, footballs, volleyball nets, badminton courts, chess, carrom, and trained physical instructors.

School Building & Infrastructure Specifications
Engineered for Lifelong Educational Safety

The school building adheres to strict PWD earthquake-resistant norms, fire-safety clearances, and universal access guidelines for inclusive education.

  • Dedicated Administrative Block with Principal's Chamber and Staff Rooms.
  • Ramps and wide corridors for accessibility (CWSN compliant).
  • Electrical backup and generator support during academic hours.
Smart Classroom Specifications
Digital Interactive Pedagogy

Fitted with high-definition interactive touch panels, curated syllabus-mapped animation modules, and digital chalkboard software to simplify complex STEM concepts.
