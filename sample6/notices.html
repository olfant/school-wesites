Admission Circular 2026-27
Urgent Directive

Commencement of Higher Secondary (+2) Registrations

Publish Date: 15 November 2026 | Ref No: SOE-VNIKETAN/ADM/2026/042

Applications are hereby invited from eligible candidates seeking admission into Class XI for the Academic Session 2026-2027 under the School of Excellence (SOE) program of the Government of Jharkhand.

Streams Offered:
  • Science Stream: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, English Core, Computer Science.
  • Arts Stream: History, Political Science, Geography, Economics, Hindi Core.
  • Commerce Stream: Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Entrepreneurship.
Candidates may obtain physical registration forms from the school administrative desk or download the official PDF guide below.
