School Management Committee

Empowering parents, teachers, and local representatives under Section 21 of the Right to Education (RTE) Act to ensure institutional accountability.

Statutory Transparency Notice: The School Management Committee is constituted under Section 21 of the RTE Act, 2009. Individual parent representative names and election tenures are designated via official placeholders pending administrative verification.
Grassroots Governance

Community-Led School Oversight

The School Management Committee (SMC) is the cornerstone of participatory governance at CM SOE VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR.

Composed of parents, local elected representatives (PRI), educators, and institutional leadership, the SMC bridges the institution with the community. At least 75% of committee members are parents or guardians, and minimum 50% are women, ensuring fair representation of community voices.

The SMC convenes monthly to inspect mid-day meals, review physical infrastructure upkeep, approve annual School Development Plans (SDP), and safeguard child protection protocols.

Statutory Composition Rules
  • 75% Parent Representation: Parents/guardians of enrolled children form the overwhelming majority of members.
  • 50% Women Mandate: Equal representation of mothers and female guardians in leadership and decisions.
  • Local Panchayat Nominees: Elected PRI ward members assist in village-level mobilization and child retention.
  • Member Secretary: Head of Institution (Principal) serves as ex-officio Member Secretary.

Statutory Duties

Roles & Responsibilities

Guiding institutional policies to guarantee equal learning rights for every child in Haldipokhar.

School Development Plan

Formulating and endorsing the annual School Development Plan (SDP) in coordination with Sarva Shiksha guidelines.

Mid-Day Meal Monitoring

Regularly inspecting meal cleanliness, nutrition, kitchen hygiene, and grain storage quality standards.

Retention & Zero Dropouts

Identifying at-risk children in the village and counseling families to prevent school dropouts, especially for girls.
