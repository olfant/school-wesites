ABOUT OUR INSTITUTION

"Every Great Journey Begins With Learning."

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is situated in the culturally vibrant and serene block of Potka, East Singhbhum, Jharkhand. As an upgraded institution of educational excellence, the school provides an encouraging, disciplined, and nurturing environment for senior secondary scholars.
