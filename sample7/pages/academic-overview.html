CURRICULAR RIGOR

Approved State Curriculum & Standards

Academics at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR follow the syllabus prescribed by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, and the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi.

The school provides instruction in both Hindi and English mediums across foundational general subjects in secondary classes, followed by specialized streams at the +2 Higher Secondary stage (Arts, Science, and Commerce).

JAC Board Aligned Continuous Evaluation Remedial Support
PEDAGOGICAL STRATEGY

Our Educational Approach

Combining traditional lecture dedication with active student participation and visual demonstrations.

Interactive Teaching

Structured chapter presentations, conceptual clarity, blackboard summaries, and regular question-answer dialogues.

Practical Demonstrations

Laboratory experiments in chemistry, biology specimens observation, physics optics, and mathematical model building.

Remedial Mentorship

Dedicated guidance for students facing difficulties in specific subjects, doubt clearance, and previous paper revisions.

CO-CURRICULAR INTEGRATION

Enriched Student Learning Activities

Academic enrichment at our school extends beyond the textbook into applied student learning initiatives:

Science & Eco Model Making Encouraging working models, solar energy displays, and environmental conservation projects.
Library & Reading Sessions Scheduled library hours to expand vocabulary, general knowledge, and literature awareness.
Quizzes & Academic Debates Classroom seminars, inter-house current affairs competitions, and speech symposiums.
