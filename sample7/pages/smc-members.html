COMMUNITY GOVERNANCE

Collaborative Leadership & Monitoring

The School Management Committee (SMC) is constituted in accordance with government educational provisions to bridge the school administration, parents, local panchayat leaders, and community stakeholders.

The committee oversees campus development, student attendance enhancement, implementation of mid-day meal norms, academic progress monitoring, and ensuring transparency in school activities.
