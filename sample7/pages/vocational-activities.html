APPLIED EDUCATION

Bridging Theory With Productive Skills

In alignment with the National Education Policy guidelines implemented across Jharkhand state schools, CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR integrates foundational vocational activities into the higher secondary curriculum.

Vocational exposure empowers rural youth with dignity of labor, hands-on craft awareness, digital productivity skills, and practical problem-solving capabilities essential for modern career opportunities.

Digital Literacy

Computer fundamentals, word processing, spreadsheet basics, and responsible internet safety.

Technical Basics

Basic household electrical safety, circuit identification, ruler measurements, and repair skills.

Horticulture & Eco-Craft

School garden plantation, soil preservation, herbal plant cultivation, and organic compost creation.

Handicrafts & Design

Waste-to-wealth model making, bamboo and jute craftwork, bookbinding, and cloth printing.

Curriculum Notice: Vocational activities are delivered as practical learning modules and co-curricular projects. Certified trade specializations correspond to official government allocations for Jharkhand schools of excellence.
