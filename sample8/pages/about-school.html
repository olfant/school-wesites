Overview

Institutional Overview & Location

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR (प्लस टू राजकीयकृत विद्या निकेतन उच्च विद्यालय) is an upgraded Higher Secondary educational institution located at हल्दीपोखर, पोटका, पूर्वी सिंहभूम, झारखण्ड – 831002.

Operating with official national U-DISE Code: 20180231602, the school serves as a beacon of academic empowerment in the East Singhbhum district. As part of the flagship CM School of Excellence initiative, the institution provides quality learning infrastructure, digital pedagogies, and comprehensive value-oriented secondary and higher secondary schooling.

Co-Educational Govt. School of Excellence Higher Secondary (+2)

Origins and Upgradation

Originally serving as a vital high school in Haldipokhar, the institution has steadily expanded its educational outreach to meet the rising aspirations of rural and suburban youth in Potka Block.

With the introduction of the CM School of Excellence model by the Department of School Education & Literacy, Government of Jharkhand, the school received substantial infrastructural and pedagogical upgrades, including smart classrooms, well-equipped science laboratories, and multi-stream +2 affiliations in Arts, Science, and Commerce.

Educational Philosophy

We firmly believe that every child has inherent intelligence and the capability to excel when provided with a nurturing, disciplined, and stimulating environment.

The goal of schooling transcends mere examination outcomes; it is about cultivating humane character, self-confidence, critical reasoning, and a lifelong thirst for knowledge.

Core Objectives

  • Deliver high-quality, inclusive education accessible to all sections of society.
  • Inculcate uncompromising discipline, ethical integrity, and civic pride.
  • Encourage experiential science inquiries, skill building, and creative expression.
  • Foster sensitivity towards environmental conservation, health, and local heritage.
Campus Facilities

Infrastructure & Amenities

A balanced physical ecosystem created to support rigorous learning and wholesome student life.

Main Academic Campus

Spacious government building with ventilated classrooms, secure boundary, and greenery.

Smart Classrooms

Equipped with digital interactive displays, educational multimedia, and modern teaching aids.

Science Laboratories

Dedicated physics, chemistry, and biology setups for mandatory hands-on practicals.

School Library

Reading corner and curated book collection of textbooks, reference works, and literature.

Playground & Sports Ground

Open field for athletic track events, football, volleyball, morning drills, and yoga.

Purified drinking water stations and separate hygienic restroom facilities for boys and girls.
