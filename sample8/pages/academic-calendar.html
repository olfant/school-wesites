Session Timeline

Annual Academic Milestones

Year-round roadmap outlining teaching terms, examination schedules, festivals, and co-curricular programs.

Term 1 (Summer & Monsoon) April – September
  • April: Commencement of academic session, textbook distributions, +2 stream orientations.
  • May – June: Summer vacation projects, tree plantation preparation, faculty orientation.
  • July: Resumption of regular classes, First Unit Evaluation tests, Scout & Guide registrations.
  • August: Independence Day celebrations (15 August), inter-house sports trials, science model planning.
  • September: Teachers' Day observances (05 Sept), Mid-Term Half-Yearly Examinations.
Term 2 (Autumn & Winter) October – March
  • October: Gandhi Jayanti, Durga Puja / Diwali breaks, regional cultural dance workshops.
  • November: Jharkhand Foundation Day (15 Nov), Annual Science & Vocational Exhibition.
  • December: Second Unit Evaluation, Annual Sports & Athletics Meet, Scout camporee.
  • January: Republic Day celebration (26 Jan), Class 10 & 12 Pre-Board examinations.
  • February – March: Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) Board Exams, annual academic results.
