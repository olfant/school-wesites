Enrolment Desk

Your Educational Journey Begins Here

Admissions to CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR are conducted strictly in adherence to the guidelines, admission schedules, and reservation policies stipulated by the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand.

Applications are invited annually for Secondary classes and Higher Secondary (+2) Science, Arts, and Commerce streams following the declaration of board matriculation results.

Step-by-Step Pathway

The 5-Step Admission Procedure

A straightforward, transparent enrolment procedure designed to assist students and parents.

01

Obtain Info

Check stream options, seat quota, and eligibility criteria on the school notice board.

02

Collect Form

Obtain the prescribed application form from the school administrative office counter.

03

Submit Docs

Fill all particulars and attach attested photocopies of previous marksheets and certificates.

04

Verification

Scrutiny of original documents by the school admission committee.

05

Confirmation

Enrolment register entry, student admission number, and section allotment.

Mandatory Document Checklist

  • Original School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (TC / SLC)
  • Self-attested photocopy of previous class Board Marksheet & Admit Card
  • Aadhaar Card copy of the student and parent/guardian
  • 4 recent passport-size photographs of candidate in school uniform
  • Residential certificate issued by competent revenue authority
  • Caste and Income Certificate for reservation claims (SC / ST / OBC / EWS)

Eligibility Criteria Overview

Secondary Admission (Class 9 - 10): Successful completion of Class 8 or Class 9 from a recognized government or affiliated school with valid Transfer Certificate.

Higher Secondary Admission (+2 Science, Arts, Commerce): Qualifying pass in JAC Class 10 Matriculation Board or equivalent recognized national board. Minimum percentage criteria for Science and Commerce are determined by the school admission committee based on annual application volumes.

Note: Admission fees, dates, and seat availability are regulated by government notifications. Do not assume admissions are currently active unless verified directly.
