Academic Curriculum

Class Structure & Subject Offerings

Affiliated with Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC), Ranchi, following the state & NCERT syllabus framework.

Class 11 & 12 (+2 Higher Secondary Level)

Students completing Class 10 Board examinations can opt for one of the three streams based on eligibility and state counselling rosters.

Stream Compulsory Subjects Elective / Core Combinations Practical & Viva Component
Science Stream Core English or Hindi, Core Language Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Computer Science / IP Physics Lab, Chemistry Lab, Biology Lab (30 Marks Practical each)
Commerce Stream Core English or Hindi, Core Language Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics, Commercial Arithmetic, Entrepreneurship Project Work, Financial Statement viva, Case Studies
Arts & Humanities Core English or Hindi, Elective Hindi/English/Regional History, Political Science, Geography, Economics, Sociology, Philosophy Geography Mapping, Project Surveys, Oral Viva
Elective Choice Guideline: Final subject combinations are assigned during admission based on student aptitude, marks in the secondary board examination, and seat capacity per section.

Class 9 & 10 (Secondary Level Curriculum)

General secondary education focused on holistic foundational mastery across linguistic, numerical, scientific, and social subjects.

Language I & II

Languages

Core STEM

Mathematics

Empirical
