Bharat Scouts & Guides

  1. Home
  2. Scout & Guide
Duty • Discipline • Service

Character Building Through Selfless Service

The Bharat Scouts & Guides unit at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is one of the most vibrant co-curricular bodies on campus.

Operating with the universal motto "Be Prepared" (तैयार रहो), scouting instills physical toughness, moral fortitude, disaster preparedness, outdoor survival acumen, and lifelong dedication to community service.

Discipline & Drill

Mastering ceremonial flag protocols, smart marching drills, uniform standards, and punctuality.

Outdoor Pioneering

Tent pitching, gadget fabrication with bamboo and ropes, knot tying, and compass navigation.

First Aid & Rescue

Bandaging techniques, stretcher making, emergency response, and community health hygiene.

Community Service

Traffic volunteer assistance, Swachh Bharat cleanliness rallies, plantation drives, and disaster relief.

Patrol System & Camporee Training

Students are organized into patrols led by elected patrol leaders. Each member progresses through standardized stages: Pravesh, Pratham Sopan, Dwitiya Sopan, and Tritiya Sopan, aiming for the prestigious Rajya Puraskar and Rashtrapati Scout & Guide commendations.

  • Weekly troop parades and inspection on the school grounds
  • Annual overnight nature survival camporee
  • Community development service projects in Haldipokhar village

Scout Promise & Law

"On my honour, I promise that I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country, to help other people at all times, and to obey the Scout Law."
