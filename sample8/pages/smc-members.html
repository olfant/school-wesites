Governance & Oversight

Community-Led Institutional Leadership

The School Management Committee (SMC) of CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR is constituted in accordance with statutory guidelines under the Right to Education (RTE) Act and the Department of School Education and Literacy, Government of Jharkhand.

The SMC functions as the primary bridge connecting the school administration, teachers, parents, and local community representatives to ensure academic discipline, infrastructural development, mid-day meal oversight, and student welfare.

Statutory SMC Responsibilities

  • Monitor school functioning and teacher attendance
  • Formulate School Development Plans (SDP)
  • Supervise utilization of government grants & funds
  • Support 100% student enrolment and retention
