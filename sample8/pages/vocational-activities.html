Hands-on Craftsmanship

Practical Skills for Real-World Self-Reliance

As part of modern educational reform under the National Education Policy and Jharkhand vocational training modules, CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR provides vocational enrichment programs.

These initiatives expose students to applied digital competencies, electrical circuits, basic agricultural technology, and local handicrafts, empowering them with practical trade awareness and self-employment confidence.

Digital & ICT Literacy

Keyboard typing, office document processing, spreadsheet accounting, and internet search ethics.

Basic Electricals & Solar

Safety handling, domestic wiring fundamentals, multimeter circuit testing, and solar panel connectivity.

Agro & Botanical Skills

Organic kitchen gardening, vermicomposting beds, sapling nursery management, and water conservation.

Handicrafts & Design

Bamboo weaving, paper bag fabrication, pottery painting, and sustainable indigenous craft techniques.

Institutional Commitment to Dignity of Labor

Vocational exposure cultivates respect for manual craft, technical ingenuity, and entrepreneurial initiative. Students learn that skill combined with academic knowledge is the true engine of rural community empowerment.

Note: Programs are conducted as co-curricular enrichment workshops. Specific NSQF-certified streams will be announced upon formal state notification.
