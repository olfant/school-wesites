Admission Information & Procedure

Comprehensive enrollment guidelines for Secondary and +2 Higher Secondary programs at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR.

Official Enrollment Verification Rule

Admission schedules, statutory eligibility rules, stream seat limits, and required government documentation are published by state educational directives. Parents and students must verify live criteria directly with the school administrative office.

STEP-BY-STEP ADMISSION PROCEDURE

How to Apply

01
Obtain Info

Review verified class eligibility, minimum age norms, and stream criteria.

02
Collect Form

Acquire official application paperwork from the school administrative office.

03
Submit Docs

Furnish previous transfer certificates, birth proof, photographs, and mark records.

04
Verification

Scrutiny and validation of documents by institutional admission scrutiny panel.

05
Confirmation

Allotment of admission roll number, section, and orientation schedule.

DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
  • Official Birth Certificate (original & photocopy)
  • Original School Leaving / Transfer Certificate (SLC/TC)
  • Marksheet & Report Card from previously attended school
  • Recent passport-size colored photographs of student
  • Aadhaar Card photocopy of student and parent/guardian
  • Category certificate (if claiming reservation / scholarship)

Admission dates coincide with the state academic calendar. Please contact the school office to confirm open intake windows.

Eligibility requires passing Class 10 Board Examinations from a recognized council in relevant subjects according to state criteria.
