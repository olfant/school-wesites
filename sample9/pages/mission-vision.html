Mission & Vision

CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR • U-DISE: 20180231602

03 INSTITUTIONAL CHARTER

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Guiding philosophies that steer the educational trajectory of CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR.

OUR VISION

Scholastic Brilliance & Moral Character

To be an exemplary center of higher secondary education where academic excellence, moral integrity, scientific mindset, and social responsibility converge to produce nation-building citizens.

  • Equitable access to top-tier secondary & higher secondary learning.
  • Inculcation of constitutional ideals, fraternity and global empathy.
  • Encouragement of scientific innovation, athletics and environmental stewardship.
OUR MISSION

Holistic Development of Every Scholar

To deliver structured, value-based and interactive education that empowers learners with academic mastery, creative problem-solving, vocational competencies and disciplined living.

  • High standard of pedagogical delivery and curriculum adherence.
  • Equal emphasis on classroom learning, laboratories and physical education.
  • Cultivation of life skills, communication and empathetic leadership.
ETHICAL PILLARS

Our Five Institutional Values

01
Scholastic Integrity

Honesty in academic exploration, authentic effort, and commitment to intellectual truth.

02
Discipline & Respect

Punctuality, mutual consideration for others, and adherence to democratic rules.

03
Inclusivity & Empathy

Valuing every student's background, promoting gender equity, and supporting peer welfare.
