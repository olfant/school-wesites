11 ATHLETICS & FITNESS

Sports & Physical Education

Physical health, endurance, and sporting discipline are central to student vitality at CM SOE UPG GOVT+2 SCHOOL VNIKETAN HALDIPOKHAR.

Under the direction of dedicated physical training educators, our students participate in outdoor athletics, track and field, and indoor strategy games.

PHYSICAL EDUCATION SPORTS ARENA
01
Track & Field

Sprints, middle-distance running, relays, long jump and high jump.

02
Outdoor Sports

Football, volleyball, kho-kho and kabaddi promoting team synergy.

03
Indoor Games

Chess and carrom developing mental concentration and analytical foresight.

04
Annual Sports Meet

Inter-house championship celebrating athleticism, fair play and sportsmanship.
